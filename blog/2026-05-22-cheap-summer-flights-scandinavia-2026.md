## What it is

Multiple airlines — SAS, Delta, and United — are pricing summer 2026 flights from US cities to Scandinavia well below what these routes normally cost. Flight deal sites have been posting these routes consistently since January, and new alerts have gone up this week.

**Confirmed deals active as of May 2026:**
- Washington DC (IAD/DCA) to Stockholm: ~$373 roundtrip via SAS
- Chicago (ORD) to Scandinavian cities: from ~$374 via SAS
- Boston (BOS) to Bergen, Norway: ~$414 roundtrip
- Portland (PDX) to Oslo area: ~$429 roundtrip
- Portland (PDX) to Copenhagen / Aalborg / Billund: $556–$597 basic economy via Delta

These aren't all from the same promotion. It's multiple airlines competing on overlapping routes. SAS is the main carrier on East Coast runs to Stockholm, Oslo, and Copenhagen; Delta has been aggressive from Portland on routes into Denmark and western Scandinavia. The deals have been documented by secretflying.com and theflightdeal.com across multiple posts throughout the year.

Travel dates: the best prices cluster in **late August and September**. Peak summer on the same routes (June–July) runs $700–$850+ from the same airports.

## Why it's worth it

The useful comparison: Washington Dulles to Stockholm in August typically costs $700–$900 roundtrip. At $373, you're saving $400+ on the ticket alone. That's the difference between a day's food and lodging budget on a 10-day trip.

This isn't a flash sale or a limited promotion. Flight deal trackers have been posting US-to-Scandinavia deals on these same routes repeatedly since January 2026 — January, March, April, and now May. The competitive pressure appears structural. SAS has been rebuilding market share aggressively since emerging from bankruptcy restructuring in 2024, and Delta's transatlantic expansion is pushing fares down from West Coast hubs. For Portland specifically, deals to multiple Denmark and Norway airports have appeared almost every week for four months.

For context: going.com's 2026 deal report listed Helsinki as one of the "most consistently cheap European destinations" from the US this year, and secretflying.com's recent US deals page has had Scandinavia entries in every weekly update.

## How to book it

Use Google Flights with flexible dates. Set your origin airport and destination region (try "Scandinavia" or individual airports like CPH, ARN, OSL) and search the full month of August. Look specifically at departure dates between **August 10 and September 10** — that's where cheap summer transatlantic dates have been clustering.

Once you find a date showing the deal price, book directly on the airline's site — flysas.com, delta.com, or united.com. Going direct avoids OTA markups and makes any changes or issues easier to resolve.

**Specific airport pairs to check:**
- IAD or DCA → CPH (Copenhagen) or ARN (Stockholm) via SAS
- BOS → CPH or OSL via SAS
- ORD → CPH via SAS or United
- PDX → CPH, AAL (Aalborg), or BLL (Billund) via Delta
- PDX → OSL via SAS

The secretflying.com deal pages use a "GO TO DEAL" button that links directly to a working search on Google Flights or Momondo — that's a useful shortcut for confirming the fare still exists before booking.

## The catch

The $373–$430 prices are basic economy or SAS Go Light fares. That means:

**No checked bag.** SAS charges EUR 20–105 per bag depending on route and when you buy — budget $55–80 if you need to check a bag. Delta basic economy is similar. Add this to your actual trip cost before deciding if the deal holds.

**No seat selection until check-in.** If you're traveling with someone and care about sitting together, upgrade to the next fare tier ($50–100 more roundtrip) or buy seat selection separately.

**You need date flexibility.** These prices don't appear on every day of August. Typically a handful of dates in any given month will show the deal price; the surrounding dates will be $200–400 more. If your schedule is locked in, run the search first before assuming this works for your specific window.

**Most routes connect.** SAS East Coast routes typically connect through Copenhagen. Delta's Portland-Scandinavia routes have one stop. Connecting times are generally 1–3 hours in a normal European hub — not brutal, but not nonstop.

Changes are allowed on most of these fares for a rebooking fee (SAS Go Light: approximately $65 per leg). These fares are not refundable.

## How long it'll last

This pricing trend has been running since at least January 2026 with no sign of reversing. Late August is historically the floor for summer transatlantic prices — demand drops off as the school year approaches — so the window to **find** these dates is probably June through mid-July. After that, the cheapest summer slots typically get picked up and prices firm.

That said, this is ongoing airline pricing, not a countdown sale. If you find a date that works, don't sit on it. Transatlantic fares at this level can reprice within 24–48 hours once availability on the cheap fare bucket runs out. Run the search, confirm the date, and book it the same session.
