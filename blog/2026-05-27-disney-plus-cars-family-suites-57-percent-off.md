## What it is

Art of Animation's Cars Family Suites run $578/night at rack rate in summer. Through Disney's Perks program — available to all Disney+ subscribers — those same rooms drop to $248–$269 per night for stays from June 21 through August 15, 2026. That's a 53–57% reduction depending on the dates.

Exact pricing by window:

- **$248–$249/night:** Most dates from June 21 to July 1, July 5–8, and July 12 to August 5
- **$269/night:** July 2–4 (Fourth of July weekend), July 9–11, and August 6–15

The Cars Family Suites sleep up to six across two connected spaces: a living area with a pull-down bed and a themed sleeping area with bunk beds. For a family of five or six, these are the only Disney Value resort rooms that don't require booking two separate hotel rooms — which is why they command a steep rack rate in the first place.

This is a meaningfully different deal from Disney's current general summer discount, which caps Value resorts at 20% off. It's also better than what the Deluxe tier gets from that same general offer. The Disney+ deal specifically targets the Family Suites — room types that are typically excluded from blanket hotel promotions.

## Why it's worth it

The $578 rack rate is the actual price you'd pay booking directly today without any promotion. Four nights for a family of five comes to $2,312 before taxes. At $259/night (the midpoint of the Disney+ range), that same trip drops to $1,036. That's over $1,200 saved on accommodations alone — real money in the context of a Disney trip where park tickets run $100–$180 per person per day.

The practical comparison: a family of five looking at Disney hotels has three options. Book a standard room that sleeps four and squeeze, book two rooms at $200+/night each, or pay for a Family Suite. Two moderate rooms at $230/night each runs $1,840 for four nights. The Cars Suite with Disney+ at $259/night runs $1,036. The suite is both cheaper and less logistically annoying.

The deal has been live since April 2, giving it genuine staying power. It's not a weekend flash sale.

## The catch

**You need Disney+.** The subscription starts at $7.99/month — if you don't have it, you'll need to subscribe first. Beyond that, you have to activate Disney+ Perks on the Disney website, which is free and takes about two minutes. Once enrolled, the discounted rates appear automatically when you search for Art of Animation Resort.

The math is still heavily favorable with a fresh subscription: the per-night savings of $300+ over rack rate makes the $7.99/month look irrelevant. But it's not a zero-friction booking — there's a prerequisite step.

**Cannot stack with other discounts.** This does not combine with the general summer hotel promotion or anything else currently running. It stands alone.

**Limited inventory.** Disney allocated a fixed number of rooms at these rates. June dates are still broadly available, but July 4 weekend and peak mid-July dates are tightening. If your family has a specific travel window, book now rather than later.

Minimum two-night stay. Maximum 14 nights.

## Where to book and when it expires

Activate Disney+ Perks at [disneyworld.disney.go.com](https://disneyworld.disney.go.com) — log in with your Disney+ account and follow the Perks enrollment prompt. Then search for Art of Animation Resort with your dates; the discounted rates load automatically for enrolled members.

The deal covers travel through **August 15, 2026**. There's no announced booking cutoff date for the promotion itself, but specific dates will sell out as summer approaches. July 4 week in particular is the highest-demand stretch — don't assume it'll stay available.
