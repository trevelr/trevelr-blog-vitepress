## What it is

IHG Hotels & Resorts is running a Third Night Free promotion on premium rooms and suites across 48 countries in Europe, the Middle East, and Africa. Book a minimum of 3 consecutive nights at the same participating property, and the third night is complimentary. The deal covers stays through September 30, 2026, and you have until September 15 to book.

It's available across IHG's full brand stack: Six Senses, Regent, InterContinental, Vignette Collection, Kimpton, Hotel Indigo, voco, Crowne Plaza, Holiday Inn, and Staybridge Suites. That range covers a lot of ground — a Kimpton boutique in Amsterdam, an InterContinental in Madrid, a Hotel Indigo in Edinburgh.

The participating country list for European travelers is comprehensive: Austria, Belgium, Denmark, England, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Ireland, Italy, Malta, Netherlands, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Scotland, Spain, Switzerland, Turkey, United Kingdom, and more — plus an extensive spread of Middle Eastern and African destinations including UAE, Saudi Arabia, Jordan, Egypt, South Africa, and Kenya.

## Why it's worth it

One free night out of three is a 33% discount. No math games — it's just a free night.

That becomes meaningful quickly at properties where nightly rates aren't trivial. A premium room at an InterContinental in Rome, Lisbon, or Madrid runs $400–$650/night in summer. A free third night on a 3-night stay at $500/night saves $500. On a 6-night stay at the same property, you get two nights free.

The deal is best suited to city trips where you're based at a single property for 3–5 nights. If you're planning an extended stay — a week in Paris, a long weekend in Istanbul, five nights in Lisbon — this is a clean 33% off the rate you'd pay anyway for a nice room.

The rates are flexible. No prepayment required, and you can cancel up to 72 hours before check-in without penalty. For a hotel deal in this savings bracket, that's worth calling out: most comparable promotions require prepayment to access the discount, which eliminates your flexibility. This one doesn't.

## The catch

The promotion is for **premium rooms and suites only**. Standard rooms don't qualify.

That restriction cuts two ways. If you were already going to book an upgraded room, this is a straightforward saving. If you'd normally book the cheapest room in the building, you'll need to upgrade first — and whether the math works depends on how much the premium tier costs at your specific property. At some hotels the gap between standard and premium is modest; at others it's $150–$250/night.

The brand range also matters more here than in most hotel promotions. IHG owns everything from Holiday Inn Express to Six Senses, and "premium room" means something very different across that spectrum. At a Holiday Inn Express, there may not be a meaningful upgrade category above standard. At a Kimpton, InterContinental, or Hotel Indigo, premium rooms are clearly defined and the deal pays off. Stick to the upper IHG tiers for this to make sense.

One more thing: the cancellation window is 72 hours, not the 24 hours you'd get at many standard flexible rates. If you need last-minute flexibility, keep that in mind.

## Where to get it and when it expires

Book at ihg.com. You'll need a free IHG One Rewards account — create one in two minutes if you don't have one. When searching your dates and property, look for the "Third Night Free" rate in the room options. The discount applies automatically to the third consecutive night.

Booking deadline: September 15, 2026. Travel must be completed by September 30, 2026. Not every property participates, so if you don't see the rate for a specific hotel, check a comparable property in the same city before assuming the deal isn't available.
