## What it is

IHG Hotels & Resorts is running a "Ready to Roam" flexible rate across 190+ properties in the Middle East, India, and Africa. The base discount is up to 25% off the standard flexible rate. IHG One Rewards members (free to join) get an additional 6% off when booking via IHG.com, or 10% off via the IHG mobile app — stacking to a maximum of 35% off at participating properties.

Eligible brands include InterContinental, Crowne Plaza, voco, Holiday Inn, and more. Eligible countries: UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman, Jordan, Egypt, Lebanon, Kuwait, India, Nepal, Mauritius, South Africa, Kenya, Tanzania, Zimbabwe, Angola, and Algeria.

Breakfast is included in the rate. Sale is valid for stays through August 31, 2026.

## Why it's worth it

A few things make this deal worth paying attention to rather than scrolling past.

**The savings are real, not padded.** InterContinental Dubai Festival City runs around $249/night at standard flexible rates. At 35% off, you're at approximately $162/night — plus breakfast, which at a five-star property typically adds another $30–45 per person per day. A couple staying four nights is looking at roughly $450 in direct savings on the room alone, plus $240–360 in breakfast value. That's meaningful.

The same math plays out in India. Crowne Plaza properties in Mumbai and Delhi regularly run $130–180/night at rack rate. With the member rate through the app, you're in the $85–120 range including breakfast. That's competitive with three-star options that don't include breakfast at all.

**Free cancellation is rare at this discount level.** Most discounted hotel rates either require full prepayment or charge a cancellation penalty. The Ready to Roam rate requires no prepayment and allows free cancellation up to three days before check-in. If your plans shift, you're not out the money.

**IHG's footprint in this region is unusually strong.** In the Middle East and South Asia, IHG brands punch above their weight relative to alternatives like Marriott or Hilton. If you're planning a trip that touches the Gulf, India, or eastern Africa in the next few months, there's a high probability an IHG property hits your target location.

## The catch

**This is a regional deal.** If you're going to Europe, Japan, Southeast Asia, or anywhere in the Americas, this does nothing for you. It applies exclusively to the countries listed above.

**The 35% requires free effort, but still effort.** To get the maximum discount, you need two things: an IHG One Rewards account (free, takes two minutes) and the IHG mobile app. If you're not already in the IHG ecosystem and book directly on a third-party site, you'll see neither discount. The base deal also only applies when you specifically select the "Ready to Roam" flexible rate — not just any available rate at those properties.

**"Up to" qualifier applies.** Not every property in the sale will hit 35% off. High-demand properties in prime locations — the InterContinental in downtown Dubai, for instance — may show shallower discounts. The full 35% is more reliably found at mid-tier IHG brands (Holiday Inn Express, voco) in secondary cities.

**Dubai in June through August is hot.** Not "uncomfortable hot" — genuinely extreme heat, regularly 40–45°C. Indoor-focused trips work fine, but if you're planning a trip where you'll be outside for long stretches, shift your focus to India (with regional exceptions for monsoon season) or eastern Africa, where June–August weather is milder.

## Where to book and when it expires

Book directly at [IHG.com](https://www.ihg.com) or via the IHG mobile app. Select the "Ready to Roam" rate when checking availability — it will appear as a distinct rate option alongside standard and advance-purchase rates. If you don't have an IHG One Rewards account, creating one on the spot is enough to unlock the full member discount; you don't need points or status.

The sale covers stays through August 31, 2026. The booking window appears to be open through the sale period, so you're not racing against an imminent cutoff — but summer availability at popular properties like InterContinental Dubai will thin out quickly. If you know your travel dates, book now and cancel later if plans change. That's exactly what the free cancellation policy is for.
