## What it is

Virgin Voyages' Spring Cruise Sale expired on May 28. The replacement kicked in May 29 and runs through July 31: the Hat-Trick deal. Three things stack together on a single booking:

1. **70% off the second sailor's fare** — same mechanic as before, which works out to 35% off per person when you run the actual math
2. **Free Sea Terrace upgrade** — you book at the Sea View cabin price, which is a step lower, and Virgin upgrades you to a Sea Terrace (a cabin with a private balcony terrace) at no additional cost
3. **$300 Sailor Loot** — $300 in onboard credit per cabin, but this piece expires June 10

The combination is meaningfully different from what ran in the spring. The spring sale applied the 70% off second sailor to whatever cabin category you were already booking. This deal gives you the discount *and* moves you into a better cabin for free.

Here's what that does to the math on a 7-night Caribbean sailing for two:

- Sea View cabin at retail: ~$900/person → $1,800 total
- 35% off per person: $585 each → **$1,170 for two**
- You're now in a Sea Terrace, which retails at ~$1,200/person → $2,400
- Effective discount vs. what the Sea Terrace would normally cost: **~51% off**
- Add the $300 Sailor Loot before June 10 and you're at $870 for two in a balcony cabin

The spring deal math got you to around 43% off a Sea Terrace booking. Hat-Trick gets you closer to 50% while also putting you in a better room.

## Why it's worth it

A few things about Virgin Voyages' pricing structure that make this comparison less straightforward than it looks:

All sailings are adults-only (18+). The base fare includes most dining, non-alcoholic beverages, basic Wi-Fi, and gratuities — no separate packages required for the most-used onboard services. Traditional cruise lines charge $60–$120/person/day for drink packages and $20–$25/person/day in gratuities on top of the sticker. On a 7-night sailing for two, that's an extra $1,100–$2,000 in tack-ons you won't be paying here.

At ~$1,170 for two on a 7-night sailing (before Sailor Loot), you're looking at about $84/person/night all-in for a balcony cabin with most costs bundled. That's competitive with mid-range all-inclusive resorts in the Dominican Republic or Jamaica, and it includes moving from port to port — the Scarlet Lady hits multiple Caribbean stops including Bimini, Puerto Plata, and the Eastern and Western Caribbean itineraries.

## The catch

**This deal only works for two.** The 70% off the second sailor requires a second sailor. Solo travelers aren't getting the couple's discount here — just the free balcony upgrade.

**Lock-it-in fares are excluded.** Lock-it-in is Virgin's non-refundable, cheapest-shown option. If you're price-sorting on the booking page and clicking the lowest number, verify it's not a Lock-it-in fare before assuming the Hat-Trick discount applies. It won't.

**The $300 Sailor Loot expires June 10 — six days from today.** The free balcony upgrade and 70% off second sailor run through July 31, but the third leg of the hat-trick is time-limited. If you want all three pieces, you have less than a week. If you need more time, the two-piece deal (35% off per person + free upgrade) is still available for another eight weeks.

**Refund window is short.** You can get a full cash refund within 7 days of booking, provided the sailing is more than 120 days out. After 7 days, the deposit converts to Future Voyage Credit (usable within a year on any Virgin sailing, but not a cash refund). Most fall and winter Caribbean sailings are far enough out to qualify for the 120-day condition. If there's any chance your plans change, plan to decide and either keep or cancel within that first week.

## How to book and how long it'll last

Book directly at **virginvoyages.com** — no promo code, the pricing should reflect the discount automatically on eligible cabin categories (Sea View and up, non-Lock-it-in fare). Filter for Caribbean sailings and look for Sea View cabins to confirm the free upgrade is showing.

Full three-piece Hat-Trick (including $300 Sailor Loot): **book by June 10, 2026** — six days.

Two-piece deal (70% off second sailor + free balcony upgrade): **book by July 31, 2026**.

Travel dates extend across fall and winter 2026 into spring 2027. You're not booking a trip for next week — you're locking in pricing now for an October or November sailing.
