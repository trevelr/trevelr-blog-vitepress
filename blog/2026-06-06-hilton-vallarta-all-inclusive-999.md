The Hilton Vallarta Riviera is a legitimate four-star property, not a budget resort dressed up in deal language. All 444 rooms are oceanfront — there's no bad view. The resort sits on a protected cove in Bahia de Banderas, which means calmer water than the more exposed beaches further north, and a backdrop of the Sierra Madre mountains behind the bay.

For context on pricing: quality all-inclusive resorts in the Puerto Vallarta region typically run $150–$300+/person/night. The Hilton's upgraded oceanfront category is consistently in that upper range when booked direct or through OTAs at non-deal rates. At $83–$167/person/night all-in, this is 40–55% below what comparable stays at this property actually cost.

The per-room pricing is what makes this unusual. Most hotel deals price per person, so groups of 3 or 4 end up paying multiples. Here, a family of 4 pays the same $999 as a couple — that's $250/person total for 3 nights of all-inclusive resort. That's hard to find at this property level.
