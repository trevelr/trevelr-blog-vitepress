## What it is

From May 28 through July 1, 2026, Chase is temporarily boosting the welcome bonuses on all three Southwest Rapid Rewards personal credit cards:

- **Southwest Rapid Rewards® Plus Credit Card** ($99/yr): 80,000 points after $1,000 spend in the first 3 months
- **Southwest Rapid Rewards® Premier Credit Card** ($149/yr): 85,000 points after $2,000 spend in the first 3 months
- **Southwest Rapid Rewards® Priority Credit Card** ($229/yr): 90,000 points after $3,000 spend in the first 3 months

Standard offers on these cards run 50,000–60,000 points. This is 30,000 points more than you'd normally get — on every card, simultaneously. Confirmed by NerdWallet and CNBC Select as of this week.

## Why it's worth it

Southwest Rapid Rewards points are worth roughly 1.3–1.5 cents each when redeemed for Wanna Get Away fares (the cheapest Southwest sells). At 1.4 cents per point:

- 80,000 points = **$1,120 in Southwest flights**
- 90,000 points = **$1,260 in Southwest flights**

Those 30,000 extra points over the standard offer are worth approximately $420 in additional flight value — just for applying now instead of three months ago.

What does that look like in practice? Southwest's Wanna Get Away fares on popular domestic routes typically run 6,000–12,000 points one-way. At 80,000 points, you can reasonably expect 6–12 one-way flights depending on route and date. Realistically, you're covering 3–5 roundtrips on the bonus alone.

The other angle worth knowing: Southwest Rapid Rewards points count toward the **Companion Pass**, one of the more underrated perks in domestic travel loyalty. Once you hit the annual qualifying points threshold, a designated companion flies free with you on every Southwest flight for the rest of the year and all of the following year — paying only the taxes. A welcome bonus in the 80,000–90,000-point range is a significant chunk of that threshold for anyone targeting the pass in 2026.

## The catch

**Southwest only.** These points are locked to Southwest's ecosystem. No transfers to hotels, other airlines, or any other program. If your travel is predominantly on Delta, United, American, or international carriers, this card is irrelevant to you. It's a deal for people who already fly Southwest or want to.

**Annual fee — applied immediately.** The fee charges when you open the card. On the Plus ($99/yr), your net bonus value in year one is roughly $1,021 after the fee. On the Priority ($229/yr), it's about $1,031 — the higher fee eats into the larger bonus, so the net year-one value across all three cards is similar. All three are positive in year one. The real question is whether you'll keep the card after that.

**Chase's 5/24 rule applies.** If you've opened five or more personal credit cards from any issuer in the past 24 months, Chase will almost certainly decline automatically. This includes cards where you're an authorized user. Check your count before applying.

**Spend requirements are real.** $1,000 in 3 months on the Plus is easy for most people — that's just regular spending. $3,000 in 3 months on the Priority card is about $1,000/month, which requires some thought. Don't apply for the Priority card if you'd need to manufacture spend to hit the threshold.

## Where to get it and when it expires

Apply at southwest.com or chase.com/southwest. Standard credit card application — instant decision in most cases.

**The offer ends July 1, 2026.** That's 15 days from today. After that, these cards revert to their standard 50,000–60,000-point offers. Southwest runs elevated bonuses occasionally, but not on a predictable schedule — this is the first time all three personal cards have hit 80,000+ simultaneously in recent memory.

If you're eligible under Chase's 5/24 rule and fly Southwest with any regularity, the math is clear and the window is closing.
