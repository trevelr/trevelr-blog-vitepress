## What it is

Chase raised the welcome offer on the Sapphire Preferred to 100,000 Ultimate Rewards points, live as of June 15. Spend requirement is $5,000 on purchases in the first 3 months from account opening. Annual fee is $95.

This is the third time in 17 years the Sapphire Preferred has reached this bonus level. The first was in 2021, and it ran about four months. The second was in 2025, and it lasted roughly one month. There's no announced expiration date on this one, but Chase is calling it limited time.

The June 15 launch also included a meaningful card refresh: 3x points on gas and EV charging (new), 3x on vacation rentals (new), a $120 Global Entry/TSA PreCheck/NEXUS application credit (new), the annual Chase Travel hotel credit bumped from $50 to $100, and a complimentary year of Apple TV+ worth up to $156.

## Why it's worth it

The standard Sapphire Preferred offer runs 60,000 points. This one is 100,000. At Chase's 1.25 cents per point redemption rate through Chase Travel, that's $1,250 toward any flight, hotel, or car rental with no blackout dates. If you optimize transfers to airline and hotel partners, 100K points is worth closer to $1,800–$2,000 depending on how you use them.

Compare that to the 60,000-point baseline: $750 in Chase Travel, or around $1,200 with solid transfers. The gap is roughly $500–$800 in usable travel value for the exact same $95 annual fee.

**What 100,000 points realistically books:**
- A round-trip economy flight to Japan via United MileagePlus (45,000 points off-peak), with 55,000 points left over for a second trip
- Two domestic round-trips in economy on United, Southwest, or British Airways Avios short-haul routes
- Multiple nights at Hyatt properties — though note the transfer ratio to World of Hyatt just changed from 1:1 to 4:3, which reduces Hyatt value slightly compared to before June 15
- $1,250 cash-equivalent toward anything in the Chase Travel portal — flights, hotels, cars, no restrictions

The new benefits also shift the ongoing math. The $120 Global Entry credit applies once every 4.5 years, but in any year you use it, it more than covers the $95 annual fee outright. The $100 Chase Travel hotel credit is annual. If you use both in the same year, you're net positive before counting a single point earned.

**The lifetime rule makes now the important moment.** In January 2026, Chase moved to a once-per-lifetime bonus structure for all Sapphire cards. If you earn the Preferred bonus today, you cannot earn it again — period — even if you cancel and reapply years from now. Getting this at 100,000 points instead of the typical 60,000 is a permanent $500–$800 difference in travel value. There is no better time to lock that in; the next window may be at a lower number.

## The catch

**$5,000 in 3 months is a real requirement.** That's roughly $1,667/month. For most households putting normal spending on the card — groceries, gas, utilities, subscriptions — it's doable. If your monthly spend is meaningfully lower, you'd need to front-load a large purchase to hit the threshold. Don't apply if you can't realistically hit it; you won't get the bonus.

**The 5/24 rule applies.** If you've opened five or more credit cards across any bank in the past 24 months, Chase will almost certainly decline the application automatically. This is not negotiable and can't be appealed.

**You can't have earned this bonus before.** Under the lifetime rule, prior Sapphire Preferred cardholders who already earned a bonus are ineligible, regardless of when that was. Check your account history before applying.

**Hyatt transfers are now 4:3, not 1:1.** For pure Hyatt redemptions, that's a real reduction in value. For everything else — United, Southwest, British Airways, Air France/KLM, Singapore Airlines — the 1:1 transfer ratio still applies.

**The 10% anniversary bonus is gone.** The card previously returned 10% of annual points earned as a bonus at renewal. That benefit was removed as part of the June 15 update. If you've held the card and relied on that perk, account for its absence.

## Where to get it and when it expires

Apply at chase.com/sapphire/preferred. Standard online application, typically instant decision for qualified applicants.

Chase has not published an end date. Based on the two prior 100K offers — four months in 2021, roughly one month in 2025 — you likely have weeks, not days. But Chase closes limited-time offers without advance notice. If you're eligible and have been considering this card, the combination of the record bonus and the once-per-lifetime rule is as strong an argument as the Sapphire Preferred has ever offered.
