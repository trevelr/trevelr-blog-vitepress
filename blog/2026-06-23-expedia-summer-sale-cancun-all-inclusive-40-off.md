## What it is

Expedia's Big Summer Sale is running through July 13, 2026, with up to 40% off select hotel stays — including all-inclusive resorts in Cancun's Hotel Zone. Featured is the Fiesta Americana Condesa Cancun All Inclusive, a beachfront property in the middle of the Hotel Zone, currently showing with free cancellation options and pricing in the low-to-mid $200s per night after discount, depending on travel dates.

The sale covers stays through December 15, 2026. No promo code needed — discounted rates load automatically when you search for participating properties on Expedia.

## Why it's worth it

The thing that makes this worth paying attention to is the combination: 40% off an all-inclusive, with free cancellation. These two things almost never appear together.

Cancun all-inclusives in the Fiesta Americana Condesa tier — beachfront, four-star, multiple restaurants, pools, and all meals and drinks included — typically run $300–$550 per night depending on travel dates and time of year. At 40% off, that window falls to roughly $180–$330 per night. For comparison, food and alcohol alone at comparable Caribbean resorts routinely adds $80–$150 per person per day when priced à la carte. The all-inclusive bundling is already doing real work before you factor in the sale.

For a five-night stay during a shoulder month — October or November, say — a couple could expect to pay $1,500–$2,750 at rack rate. Under the sale, that same stay comes out closer to $900–$1,650, everything included.

The refundability angle is the sleeper here. All-inclusive deals almost always require upfront, non-refundable payment. The free cancellation filter on Expedia flips that: you can book now, lock in the sale rate, and cancel without penalty if plans change. That's not standard for this deal type.

## How to book it

Go to Expedia.com → Hotels → search Cancun → use the "Free Cancellation" filter in the left sidebar. The Fiesta Americana Condesa and other participating all-inclusives appear in results with sale rates already applied.

If you're flexible on property, browse the full filtered results — this sale covers multiple all-inclusive properties in the Hotel Zone, not just one. The strongest discounts tend to show on weekday check-ins and shoulder-season travel dates from September through November.

The booking deadline is July 13, 11:59 p.m. at the property's local time.

## The catch

"Up to 40% off" is the ceiling, not the guarantee. Peak dates — late December, US holiday weekends, spring break windows — will show smaller discounts or none at all. The deal is strongest for September through November travel, where you're stacking the sale discount on top of lower base rates.

The free cancellation option isn't blanket. When you select a property, double-check that the specific rate you're booking carries the "refundable" or "free cancellation" label — some non-refundable rates will appear alongside refundable ones, usually priced a bit lower. Don't assume; look for the label.

Blackout dates exist. Even within the December 15 travel window, certain high-demand dates may be excluded. Check the terms on the specific listing before you check out.

One more honest note on all-inclusives generally: the Fiesta Americana Condesa is well-reviewed for this category, but "well-reviewed all-inclusive" means the food is good, not that it's going to rival a standalone restaurant. Know what you're booking — convenience and value bundling, not fine dining.

## How long it'll last

Booking deadline is July 13 — 20 days from today. After that, rates revert to standard pricing. The travel window extends through December 15, so the urgency is about booking, not about when you actually go.

Inventory at the top properties will thin before the deadline. The Fiesta Americana books heavily in October and November once snowbird season picks up demand. If you're planning a fall trip and this is the property you want, don't wait until the last week of the sale.
