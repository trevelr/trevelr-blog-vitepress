## What it is

The Delta SkyMiles Gold American Express Card is sitting at a 90,000-mile welcome bonus right now, and it expires July 15, 2026. The bonus is structured in two tiers: 70,000 miles after $3,000 in purchases in the first six months, plus 20,000 more after an additional $2,000 in the same window. Full bonus requires $5,000 total in six months.

No annual fee in year one. After that, $150 per year.

## Why it's worth it

The standard offer on this card when nothing is happening runs between 40,000 and 70,000 miles — it floats around depending on the season. At 90,000 miles, this is noticeably above that range, and the gap is the whole point.

Here's what 90,000 SkyMiles actually buys you right now:

**Hawaii.** Round-trip economy from the mainland to Hawaii in shoulder season runs about 30,000–40,000 SkyMiles in Delta's dynamic pricing. Find the low end and 90,000 miles covers two round trips. Find the high end and you've got one comfortable round trip with a buffer.

**The Caribbean.** East Coast to Aruba, St. Maarten, or the Dominican Republic in economy prices out at 20,000–35,000 miles round-trip in off-peak windows. Three Caribbean trips from one welcome bonus is realistic if you're booking outside peak holiday weeks.

**Transatlantic economy.** Delta's awards to Europe are variable — honestly, some of the most variable in the business — but economy to London, Amsterdam, or Paris from major US gateways can clear at 30,000–60,000 miles round-trip on dates that aren't July 4th or Christmas. If you're flexible on timing, 90,000 miles is enough for a round trip to Europe. If you're not flexible, don't count on it.

**Pay with Miles.** When award availability doesn't cooperate, Delta's Pay with Miles option applies miles as a statement credit against revenue fares at 1 cent per mile. 90,000 miles = $900 off Delta flights. Not exciting, but reliable.

On the card itself: 2x miles on Delta, restaurants, and U.S. grocery stores. Free first checked bag for you and up to eight companions on the same booking — worth $35 per person each way. Two domestic round trips per year with a single checked bag generates $140 in bag-fee savings. At a $150 annual fee, the card effectively pays for itself in that one benefit if you fly Delta at all regularly.

## The catch

**Delta SkyMiles don't have a fixed award chart.** Delta eliminated its award chart years ago. The prices in the section above are real, but they're snapshots — the same route can show 28,000 miles on a Tuesday in October and 90,000 miles on a Friday in July. This isn't a theoretical risk, it's how the program works. Before you bank on a specific trip, search it in the Delta app to see what it actually prices at.

**The two-tiered spend adds up.** You need $3,000 spent to trigger the first batch, then another $2,000 to get the full 90,000. That's $833/month for six months. If your typical card spending runs lower than that, you'd need to front-load specific purchases or use the card for regular bills to hit the threshold.

**The annual fee kicks in at month 13.** The $0 first-year offer is real. The $150/year renewal is also real. If Delta isn't your primary airline or you're not getting value from the bag perk or 2x categories, cancel or downgrade before the fee posts. Set a calendar reminder when you apply.

**Welcome bonus is for new Delta Gold cardmembers only.** If you've held the Delta SkyMiles Gold card before or currently have it open, you're likely ineligible. Amex also generally limits welcome bonuses to once per card in a lifetime under their current rules. Read the fine print before applying if you've had AmEx Delta cards before.

## Where to get it and when it expires

Apply directly through delta.com or americanexpress.com. Standard application — instant decision in most cases, though Amex occasionally requests documentation and takes a few days.

This offer expires July 15, 2026. That's 21 days. There's no indication it will extend, and elevated Delta card offers have historically come and gone without announcement. If the math works for you, don't assume it'll still be there next week.
